Rhythm Reader

Hold up your phone while a song plays, and Rhythm Reader tells you which line dance rhythm it's danced to, with the BPM, the meter, and how sure it is.

How a call is made

  1. Identify the song. Shazam recognizes the track playing in the room.
  2. Check the personal library. Songs already confirmed on the dance floor return instantly.
  3. Search CopperKnob step sheets. A published dance tagged Cha Cha, WCS, or Waltz settles the question.
  4. Look up tempo and time signature. Measured BPM and meter come from GetSongBPM.
  5. Weigh the evidence. Claude combines the sources and returns a rhythm, confidence, and runner-up picks.
  6. Confirm or correct. One tap saves the answer. Corrections are kept so similar songs get called better next time.

Data sources

Tempo and time signature data provided by GetSongBPM.com.
GetSongBPM

Measured BPM and time signature for each recognized song, used to separate 3/4 rhythms from 4/4 and to check tempo.

CopperKnob

Published line dance step sheets. A dance's level or title often names its rhythm outright.

Shazam

Song recognition through the iOS Recognize Music action.
